### Decks 2.13.0 — a calmer review screen

- The review screen is one card: the question, a line in your accent colour, then the answer. Header, progress line, card and buttons share one column in a modal, a tab or a narrow split pane.
- The header shows the deck name and one line such as "0 of 15 · 24:54 left". Undo sits at the end of that line.
- Card text sizes itself: short cards get larger text, longer ones step down until the card fits. The question no longer moves when the answer appears.
- Open note, read aloud, scratchpad, copy, bury, suspend and reset are in one menu under the cog at the card's top right.
- The note's heading path sits at the top of the card. The cloze number, tags and the leech warning sit at its bottom left.
- Notes open in a panel over the card instead of a section under the answer. They show markdown, maths, code and HTML.
- Template cards show inside the same single card.
- Browse: the slider is replaced by the progress line under the header. Click or drag it to jump to a card.
- Exams use the same layout: one card per question, and a progress line with one segment per question that you can click to jump to it.
- "Show answer" and "Time remaining" are now in sentence case.
