### Decks 2.12.4 — recover lost review history

- Settings → Backup → Rebuild card scheduling now also finds reviews left behind when a card's id changed, for example after its front was edited or it moved to another deck. It attaches them to the card with the same content and restores that card's scheduling. It only does this when exactly one card matches.
- The notice reports how many cards got their history back and how many had their scheduling restored.
- The fix reaches your other devices through sync once they run Decks 2.12.4 or DecksMD 1.3.3. Run Rebuild from history once on each device after updating.
- Resetting a card or a deck also clears reviews left behind under the card's old id, so a later rebuild doesn't bring them back.
