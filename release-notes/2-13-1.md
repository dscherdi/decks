### Decks 2.13.1 — an index card on large screens

- On large screens the review card is a 4:3 index card in the middle of the review. Text too long for it scrolls inside the card. Phones and narrow panes keep the full-height card.
- A card counts as mature once its stability reaches 21 days, the same rule as the mobile app. Mature counts in the deck list and statistics can shift slightly.
- A card rated Good after Again in a session now waits for its own due time instead of coming straight back.
- Mermaid diagrams with `%%` comments or `---` front matter stay whole on cards and in notes, also after editing a card's answer.
