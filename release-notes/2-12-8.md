### Decks 2.12.8 — phone windows inside the safe areas

- On phones, Decks windows stay inside the screen's safe areas. Before, their top rows could sit under the status bar and their bottom buttons under the home indicator.
- The close button of a Decks window on a phone is smaller and sits in the top corner of the window's first row. The empty strip it took above the content is gone.
- In the review window on a phone, the gap below the answer buttons is smaller: the bottom safe area was added twice.
