### Decks 2.12.1 — exercise fixes

- Exam exercises now get their marker, as other cards do, so an exercise keeps its review history when its heading is reworded.
- Exporting a directory package reads each note again first, so a package exported right after updating Decks includes its exercises with their questions.
- The export stops with an error when a note cannot be read, instead of packaging an older copy of its cards.
