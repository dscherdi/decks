### Decks 2.12.9 — a save can no longer remove the database

- Fixed: two saves could start at the same moment, most often when Obsidian on a phone went to the background, and one of them could remove the flashcard database file. The next start then began with an empty database, and in a large vault Obsidian on the phone could keep restarting.
- Saves now run one at a time, and a save that cannot finish leaves the existing database file in place.
- If this happened to you: open the vault on a device that still shows your decks and schedules and let it sync before opening it on the phone, or use Restore backup in the Decks settings.
