### Decks 2.12.7 — clozes by line, lighter start, deck list and migration fixes

- Profiles have a Group clozes by line setting, shown while clozes are on. With it on, the clozes on one line are hidden, revealed and rated together in review, cram and browse, and each keeps its own schedule. Undo takes the whole line back; suspend, bury and reset apply to every card of the line.
- The plugin reads less when it starts. The DecksMD app's database and other devices' sync logs are read again only after they change, and the flashcard database is no longer copied in memory while it loads and saves. This matters most in vaults with a large flashcard database.
- The deck list updates on its own when a deck is created, renamed or removed, and when a note gets its deck tag in a later save. Before, it kept the old list until Refresh.
- A note with two deck tags is one deck under the more specific tag. Before, its deck's tag and profile could change from one sync to the next.
- Renaming a note that is a deck no longer fails with "FOREIGN KEY constraint failed".
- Spaced Repetition migration: the scan line, the result line and the notice report the same numbers, counting one card per direction of a reverse card and per cloze highlight.
- Spaced Repetition migration: running it again keeps the schedule of cards reviewed in Decks since the first run.
- Spaced Repetition migration: a note with inline cards no longer gives its review note the schedule of its last card, and the decks of migrated notes appear in the panel without Refresh.
- Import from Anki carries over each review's answer time, so imported reviews count toward review time totals. Reviews imported earlier keep 0.
- Settings show a Google Play badge for the Android app beside the App Store badge.
