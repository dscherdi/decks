### Decks 2.12.5 — deck directory in the Decks panel, new-card order

- The store button in the Decks panel header opens the deck directory: its free decks, with search, subject and language filters, and sorting by newest, downloads or likes.
- Add puts a deck in your vault straight away. When a newer version of a deck you have is published, it shows Update, and your progress is kept. Added and Updates list only your decks from the directory.
- Selecting a deck shows its description, the decks inside, version, size and licence.
- On a fresh install the Decks side panel opens once.
- Profiles have a New card order setting: in note order, in order added (the default) or random. In note order, new cards come in the order the note lists them, including cards added to the middle of a note later.
- An exam whose typed answers are too long to check automatically now opens set to self-grading and says why, instead of offering no questions.
- Self-graded typed answers get a multi-line box. Enter adds a line; Cmd/Ctrl+Enter submits.
- Image occlusion and exam cards are no longer marked as dense.
- Changing a profile's header levels, cloze or exam setting, or assigning a profile to a tag, now re-reads the affected notes when the profiles window closes instead of after a full refresh.
- Exported Decks packages keep cards in the order the note lists them, and suggested directory names spell out umlauts and ß (ä → ae, ß → ss).
