### Decks 2.10.2 — AI generator progress and the card panel

- A running round says what it is doing: reading, thinking with the seconds counting, or the card being written. Show thinking opens the model's reasoning where the provider sends it.
- After 45 seconds without a new card, the round says so and offers Stop. A provider that stops answering ends the round with an error, and a busy or over-quota reply is reported instead of being sent again.
- PDF selections over 8 pages are generated a section at a time, so the first cards arrive before the whole selection is read.
- New setting for Decks Pro, PDF reading: Auto (the default) uses a page's own text where it reads cleanly and transcribes the rest; Always transcribe reads every page as before.
- Generated cards with broken formatting are repaired as they arrive. What is left carries a formatting chip, and Fix formatting repairs one card or all of them.
- Every round has Discard all next to Keep all, and a discard can be undone for a few seconds.
- Rounds show in the thread as a short preview with their counts and first fronts. Opening one shows its cards in the side panel, which switches between that round and all cards and filters flagged or kept ones.
- The side panel is one resizable column for every view, opens on Cards once there are cards, and is a full-screen sheet on narrow screens.
- Concept extraction reads a few pages at a time with a progress bar and Stop, and continues with the pages not read yet.
- A rewritten card is read correctly when the model bolds its labels or leaves out the delimiter, instead of taking text from the next card into the answer.
