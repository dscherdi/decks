### Decks 2.12.2 — shared folders with the DecksMD app

- Decks from the deck directory added in the DecksMD app now appear in Obsidian, with the reviews done in the app. Obsidian picks them up when it starts and when its window comes back into focus.
- Removing such a deck in the app removes it in Obsidian too.
- Images, audio and video of deck directory packages are kept in a "Decks media" folder at the vault root, which the DecksMD app uses too, so they reach every device with the vault.
- PDF OCR text is kept in "Decks OCR" and copies of PDFs attached to AI sessions in "Decks AI sources", both at the vault root. Files from the earlier hidden locations move there on the first start.
- Settings → File locations can point each of these folders elsewhere.
